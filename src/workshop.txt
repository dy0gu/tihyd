description=TIHYD (/taɪd/) is a mod that focuses on adding fully configurable character aging functionality to Project Zomboid.
description=
description=Your character will now age and become [b]older[/b] as he survives the apocalypse, with a peaceful death now as an option!
description=
description=This mod is open source. You can view the [url=https://github.com/dy0gu/tihyd]source code on GitHub[/url] to see how it works.
description=
description=▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂▂

description=[h1]Features[/h1]
description=[list]
description=[*] Your character ages as you survive.
description=[*] Your fitness and strength XP decline faster as you get older.
description=[*] Death by old age becomes possible, influenced by your character's current health and age.
description=[*] Your hair becomes gray!
description=[*] You can become short-sighted or hard of hearing.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]NEW:[/b] You can now choose your starting age on the character creation screen and see your current age on the info panel!

description=
description=The mod is translated into all languages supported by the game.
description=
description=Use the sandbox settings to:
description=[list]
description=[*] Choose how long it takes to age a year, for faster aging playthroughs.
description=[*] Enable or disable death by old age and set the minimum age at which it can happen.
description=[*] Choose whether traits (good & bad) affect the chance of dying of old age.
description=[*] Set when gray hair, short-sightedness or hearing loss appear, or disable them.
description=[*] ...and more!

description=[h1]Compatibility & Extensibility[/h1]
description=
description=TIHYD is designed for compatibility and can even interact with other mods. [b]Place it as early as possible in your load order.[/b]
description=

description=[list]
description=[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3589548354]Improved Hair Menu Build 42[/url] - Functional, but hides the age selection field in character creation, so your character spawns with a random age.
description=
description=[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3488600400]TwisTonFire - Better Character Info[/url] - Functional, but hides your current age on the character info panel, so you have to find another way to track it.

description=
description=If you find an incompatible mod, please report it in the discussions or on GitHub using the links below.

description=[/list]
description=

description=[h1]F.A.Q[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Can I install/uninstall this on an existing save?[/b]
description=
description=Yes, you can. Installing on an existing save will give your character a random age value from the range of possible values in character creation. If you uninstall the mod, your character will stay at their current age and won't suffer any more age-related effects. Traits gained from aging, such as Hard of Hearing, will remain on your character. Reinstalling the mod will resume age progression.
description=
description=[b]Does this work in multiplayer?[/b]
description=
description=This mod has not been tested for multiplayer with B42. It should work, but I would appreciate it if you could test it and report back so I can update this section.
description=
description=[b]How do I set my starting age?[/b]
description=
description=During character creation, there will be a new [b]Age[/b] field which lets you choose the starting age for your character.
description=
description=[b]Can I set my birthday date?[/b]
description=
description=Your birthday is the day your character is created in the world.
description=
description=If I let you pick an actual day, month and year, you could create a 70-year-old character on a multiplayer server that has been running for 50 years. They would be 120 years old and would probably die instantly on spawn.
description=
description=[b]How can I see my current age while playing?[/b]
description=
description=Current age can be seen at the bottom of the character info panel.
description=
description=[b]Is death by old age instant, or is there a warning?[/b]
description=
description=Death is essentially instant once your character rolls the chance to die. A message appears above their head saying they died of old age, so you're not left wondering what happened.
description=
description=[b]What is the performance hit?[/b]
description=
description=Aside from the initialization, there is only a script running [b]once per in-game day[/b], so your performance will remain untouched.
description=
description=[b]Can I dye my hair after it turns gray/white?[/b]
description=
description=Of course! Just remember that, like hair growing back in vanilla, it will regain its natural gray/white color after some time.
description=
description=[b]Are these actual questions that people asked or did you just write them all yourself?[/b]
description=
description=Yes.
